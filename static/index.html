📖 Teacher Quick-Start Guide

1. Upload Study Material

Upload a textbook chapter or notes in PDF or Word (.docx). The pipeline extracts educational facts and automatically filters out ISBNs and metadata.

2. Specify Target Content

Target specific chapters (e.g. 1, 3) or topics. You can also click detected heading chips to add them directly.

3. Set Difficulty Balance

Adjust Bloom's taxonomy levels between Easy, Medium, and Hard. The total must equal 100%.

4. Design Sections Blueprint

1-mark questions can be multiple choice (MCQ) or short answers. 2+ marks are descriptive. The system automatically verifies mark weights and question counts.
